About this system

Source triangulation: Items are classified into five source classes (primary, operator, practitioner, media, market) and scored on five credibility axes: proximity to the claim, incentive alignment, reproducibility, adoption signal, and time decay.

Hype detection: Items where language intensity is high but evidence density is low are flagged. They are not removed — they are labelled explicitly, because hype is itself a signal about what narratives are being amplified.

Trend states: Each theme is classified as emerging, scaling, mature, or declining based on volume velocity, cross-source diversity, and adoption proxies. A media-only spike does not qualify as a trend.

Sources: YouTube (practitioner), Hacker News (practitioner), Substack newsletters (media), RSS feeds (varies). arXiv, Hugging Face, and operator changelogs are added incrementally.
